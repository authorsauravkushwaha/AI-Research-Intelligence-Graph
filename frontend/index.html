A knowledge graph of AI research — with the algorithms shown

NEXUS builds a graph of papers, topics, methods, datasets and claims, then runs graph algorithms over it: similarity, community detection, centrality and link prediction feed a transparent research-gap engine and a GraphRAG agent. Every number is traceable to the records that produced it.

Prototype, not oracle

The NEXUS Opportunity Score is a prototype heuristic (30% community separation, 25% semantic similarity, 20% relationship sparsity, 15% research activity, 10% bridge potential). Results are hypotheses derived from a curated demo corpus — verify them at the sources.

Ask the research agent

      

        The agent plans tool calls over the graph, retrieves a GraphRAG context, then answers
        with the evidence attached. Without an LLM key it answers from graph evidence with
        deterministic templates — the numbers and citations stay real either way.
      

      

        
        
      

      

      

      

    

  


  
  

    

      
Research Opportunity Report

      

        Assembles the same evidence the other views show into a reviewable document
        (markdown / JSON). Deterministic by default; if an LLM key is configured, its
        executive summary is labelled as such.
